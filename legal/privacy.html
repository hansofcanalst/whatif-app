2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Photos you upload. When you choose a photo to transform, that photo is processed by the App and transmitted to Google's Gemini API. If you are signed in, the original photo and each generated result are stored in our Firebase Cloud Storage bucket so they remain available in your gallery across sessions and devices.
  • Account information. If you create an account, we store the email address, display name, and (where applicable) profile picture associated with your sign-in method, including information provided through "Sign in with Apple" or Google. We do not receive your Apple ID password or Google password.
  • Generation history. We store a record of each generation you create — including the selected category and subcategories, the prompt variant used, whether server-side people detection flagged any person as appearing to be a minor, and the time of the request. This lets us populate your gallery and reconstruct the context if a moderation or support issue arises.
  • Subscription state. If you subscribe to a paid tier (WhatIf Pro), we store your subscription status, plan, and renewal state. Payment itself is processed by Apple, Google, or our payments partner (see Section 4); we never receive or store your full payment card number.
  • Communications. If you contact us for support or send feedback, we keep your messages and contact details so we can respond and improve the product.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Operational telemetry. The Service logs per-generation outcomes — success, failure, duration, the prompt variant used, retry attempts, and detection results — so we can monitor reliability, fix bugs, and tune prompt quality. These logs are tied to your account's internal user ID but do not contain the photos themselves or any free-text content you provided.
  • Crash and error reports. When the App encounters an unhandled error, a stack trace and diagnostic context (such as device model, OS version, App version, and an opaque user identifier) are sent to our error monitoring vendor (Sentry) so we can diagnose and fix the issue. Crash reports do not include your email, display name, or photos.
  • Device and technical information. When the App communicates with our servers, we receive standard request metadata such as IP address, device type, and approximate location (city-level, derived from IP) for the limited purposes of security, abuse prevention, and operating the Service.

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not sell your personal data. We do not display third-party advertising in the App. We do not track you across other apps or websites for advertising purposes (no IDFA / cross-app tracking). We do not access your camera roll beyond the specific photos you pick to upload, and we do not access your contacts, microphone, or location services.

  • respond to your support requests and other communications;

Legal basis (EEA / UK users). 5. Where your data is stored

Our backend runs in the United States, on infrastructure operated by Google (Firebase) in the us-central1 region. The Gemini API processes requests on Google-operated infrastructure, which may include locations outside the country in which you reside.

If you are accessing the App from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with data-export restrictions, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Where required by law, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses) put in place by our sub-processors.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and content. We keep your account information, uploaded photos, generated results, and gallery for as long as your account remains active.
  • Deletion. When you delete your account from within the App (Profile → Delete Account), we delete your Firestore records, stored photos and generations, and your Firebase Authentication record. This action is irreversible.
  • Operational and security logs. Anonymized telemetry and security logs may persist for a limited period (typically up to 90 days, longer where required for security or legal reasons) after account deletion. These logs do not contain personally identifying content.
  • Moderation records. If a generation was refused for safety reasons (for example, the presence of a person detected as a minor in a sensitive category), the moderation record may be retained longer for safety auditing, with personal data minimized.
  • Legal holds. We may retain information for longer when required to comply with a legal obligation, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

8. Children and minors

WhatIf is rated 17+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

As an additional safety measure, the App's server-side people detection automatically refuses generations in the most identity-sensitive categories (race swap, gender swap, and the ethnicity-blend premium category) whenever any person visible in the uploaded photo is detected as appearing to be under 18. This check runs on our servers and cannot be bypassed from the client. A record of refusals is retained for safety auditing.

9. Security

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a security incident that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
